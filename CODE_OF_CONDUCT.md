# Code of conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing someone's private conversations are unacceptable.

Use synthetic examples when discussing bugs or style analysis. Do not pressure contributors or
users to share private data. Participation does not grant permission to reuse their messages.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct harms
the project or its users. Apply decisions consistently and explain them when doing so is safe.
For concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository's available private channel; if none
is listed, request private contact in an issue without naming affected people or exposing details.
